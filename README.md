Hi everyone! This is the 90 day detox tool, which is targeted for those who are really struggling with a video game addiction and are trying to curb it. The idea of the 90 day detox is to cut out video games completely for 90 days while your brain gets adjusted to the dopamine 
levels, since gaming is a very overstimulating activity. This tool is now available to use! To use this application, you must first install Java 8 or JRE 25 which can be done from the Oracle website. After that, simply download the app and run the file. 

**Who is this app for?**

This app is for those who are struggling with video game addiction and want to try out the 90 day detox. To get you up to speed, the 90 day detox is a tool that is very effective for fighting a video game addiction. In essence, you start off by taking a 90 day break from gaming and after the 90 days is over, you decide what your next steps are. It is essentially a grace period to allow your dopamine levels to reset back to baseline so that you can start living the life that you can start living a more happy and fulfilling life.

**How do I use it?**

There are a few features that are implemented in this application. One of them is a journaling tool, and the other is the detox tracker. The idea is that when you quit gaming, you want to start the detox tracker so you can keep track of how many days you have quit. It sort of quantifies the process so that you can see your progress in real time. You can simply do this by clicking "Track your 90 Day Detox" and "Start detox". You can also set a manual date for your detox start if you didn't start your 90 day detox at this time. Once you fail the detox, simply click "stop detox". 

Another key feature of this application is the ability to journal. One of the most important parts of the 90 day detox is journaling so that you can keep track of your thoughts and emotions. Quitting gaming is something that is extremely difficult, and you might have many withdrawal symptoms (at least I did). Thus, it is important to note down all the ups and downs that you are going through during the process. Journaling in general is quite helpful but especially for the 90 day detox it is extremely vital. To create a journal entry, click "Create a new journal entry" and then note down the date and your journal entry. You can also edit your journal entries by going to "Edit current journal entries" and you can create, read, update, and delete any journal entries you desire.
